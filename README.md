# English Cartomancy in the New Mage's Guild Cartomantic System

## A Game of Dynamical Divinatory Compute

**Thalia Ephemera** & **Urania Ephemera** — with **Gaius Jocundus** — 2026

---

This is a fresh, focused study of English playing-card cartomancy and the
recursive Mage's Guild method that grew around it. English cartomancy is the
normative worldly system. The Marseille Minor Arcana appears only as an
extension: it may add compatible structure, geometry, and detail, but it never
overrides English meanings.

The method treats cards as a discoverable symbolic-computational instrument:
questions become inputs, positions become registers, expansions become
recursive calls, ordered card sets become reversible programs, and readings
become traces through a bounded phase-space.
